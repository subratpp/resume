\begin{rubric}{Research Statement}
\begin{justifying}  % Requires 
I am broadly interested in \textbf{Trustworthy Reinforcement Learning}, particularly in designing interpretable, robust, and verifiable policies for safety-critical domains. My current work focuses on \textbf{Neurosymbolic} approaches such as programmatic policies (such as differentiable decision trees), which combine explicit knowledge representation with learning and aim to bridge fast, intuitive (System 1) behaviors with deliberate, symbolic (System 2) reasoning.
\end{justifying}
\end{rubric}
